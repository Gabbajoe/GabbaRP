GabbaRP — Line Pack Builder

Compose flavor lines for every skill, Food & Drink, Death Reactions, and Group Greetings in a proper editor, in English and (if you use it) your Local Language, then generate a text block to paste into the in-game Import box.

Nothing here touches your account or sends anything anywhere. It's a text generator that runs entirely in your browser. Default lines are generated directly from the addon's own source, so they always match what's actually shipped.

Skills

Each card starts pre-filled with the shipped default lines. Edit, add, or remove lines freely, "Reset" restores just that skill. The Local Language block ships empty for every skill except Mind Control/Mind Vision Whisper, since GabbaRP ships English-only by default.

Food & Drink

The combined one fires instead of the individual lines when both buffs are up at once.

Death Reactions

Group/Raid fire in your party/raid chat when someone in your group dies. Guild always fires in Guild chat, via the optional DeathNotificationLib addon, whenever any guildmate dies, whether you're grouped with them or not.

Group Greetings

"Join" fires once when you join a group; "Welcome" greets each new member by name (use %t), both split by time of day.

Export

Generates a block for everything currently shown above (the selected class + Food & Drink + Death Reactions + Greetings, English and Local Language both). Paste the result into the in-game "Import" box.
